CCH GraphBLAS Walkthrough

A walkthrough of the design proposal for the CCH index on GraphBLAS: the structures it adds, how the build makes them from the graph, and how they live across versions under MVCC. One small road network carries every step, and every number on the page comes from a model of the design. How the algorithm itself works is in the CCH explainer; this page does not repeat it, and keeps the step-by-step detail on the example in the appendix.

The design is 03-design.md. The numbers come from the model script walkthrough.py and its output example.json; nothing here is measured.

Related pages: the team document on the redesign, CCH redesign, and the CCH explainer, which walks through the algorithm on today’s code.
